Lumina

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumina ("the app", "we", "our"), developed by Abdulmajidkhan007, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app. Lumina is a social app for sharing posts, photos, reels, and messages with other users.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide Lumina's core social features:

CategoryExamples
Account information Email address and password, used to create and authenticate your account.
Profile information Display name, username, bio, and profile photo that you choose to add.
User content Posts, photos, reels/videos, comments, and direct messages you create or send within the app.
Device push token A token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can deliver notifications (e.g. new likes, comments, or messages) to your device.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data described above is stored using Google Firebase infrastructure, specifically:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages your account credentials and sign-in sessions.
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile information, posts, comments, and messages.
  • Firebase Cloud Storage — stores photos, videos, and other media you upload.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — stores your device push token to deliver notifications.

We do not operate any separate servers of our own — Firebase is the sole backend provider for Lumina.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate the app's core functionality, including:

  • Creating and securing your account.
  • Displaying your profile, posts, reels, and comments to other users as you configure (public or private).
  • Delivering direct messages between you and other users.
  • Sending push notifications about activity relevant to you.
We do not show ads, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You are in full control of your account and can request deletion at any time:

  • In-app deletion: Open Lumina and go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile data and your Firebase Authentication record.
  • By email: If you are unable to use the in-app option, email santexnika.atoyo@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we will delete your account and associated data.

See our Account Deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

5. Children's Privacy

Lumina is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below so we can remove it.

6. Data Security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure, including encryption in transit and access rules that restrict data to authenticated users, to help protect your information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Lumina after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
