How to Delete Your Account

Applies to the Lumina mobile app

You can permanently delete your Lumina account and all associated data directly from within the app, in under a minute.

In the app

  1. 1Open Lumina and go to your profile.
  2. 2Tap Settings.
  3. 3Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. 4Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, your profile, posts, photos, reels, messages, and authentication record are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile information (username, display name, bio, avatar).
  • Your account's authentication credentials (email/password sign-in).
  • Your device push notification token.

Content you shared that involves other users (e.g. messages sent to another person's inbox, comments on other users' posts) may remain visible to those users as part of their own conversation history, consistent with how the app's messaging and commenting features work.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to sign in or use the in-app option, email us at santexnika.atoyo@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, and we will delete your account and data on your behalf.

For details on what data we collect and how it's used, see our Privacy Policy.
